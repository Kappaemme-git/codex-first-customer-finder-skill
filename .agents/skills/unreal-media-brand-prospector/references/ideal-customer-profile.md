# UMG ideal customer profile

Primary prospects are active consumer or visually marketable brands with recurring paid-social, short-form, UGC, product-imagery, or launch needs; a credible professional buying path; and no material legal, safety, or practical disqualifier.

Increase confidence for recent launches, multiple SKUs, active advertising or creators, frequent promotions, retail expansion, growth/creative hiring, seasonal needs, premium positioning, and strong products with an observable neutral creative gap.

Reject inactive or unclear businesses, unsupported or prohibited categories, misleading claims, counterfeit/IP risk, competitors without a partnership rationale, stale directory-only evidence, implausible budget/use case, existing clients, suppressed records, and candidates with no current signal.
