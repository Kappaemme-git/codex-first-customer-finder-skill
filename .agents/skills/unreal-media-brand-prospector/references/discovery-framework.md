# UMG discovery framework

Search several buckets instead of generic brand lists:

1. Product/launch: collection, restock, packaging, SKU, crowdfunding, retail expansion.
2. Marketing: paid social, creators, ambassador programs, launch countdowns, frequent short-form.
3. Creative need: repetitive assets, sparse lifestyle content, limited formats, catalog/content mismatch. Describe neutrally.
4. Growth/timing: funding, hiring, leadership, distribution, geography, partnerships, site relaunch.
5. Explicit demand: public requests for product photography, product video, UGC, paid-social creative, production capacity, or help with a content bottleneck. When the campaign excludes `ugc_ad`, preserve UGC-ad evidence as an exclusion instead of treating it as a qualifying opportunity.

Use official websites, product/press pages, public social profiles, jobs, ad libraries, crowdfunding, retail announcements, interviews, and permitted business sources. Search snippets are not evidence.
