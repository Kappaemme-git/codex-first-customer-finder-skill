# UMG deduplication rules

Use the shared canonical domain, normalized names, aliases, social handles, parent, subsidiary, and subbrand relationships. A prior company is never new.

A prior company can be re-engagement only when enabled, a new dated source and reason are recorded, evidence is fresh, cooldown permits it, the source is not already in history, and the result is labeled `existing_new_trigger` with `reengagement: true`. Test-only cooldown overrides are prohibited in real run reports.

Distinct subbrands need independent identity and opportunity while retaining their parent link. Suppression, active-client, active-partner, and active-outreach controls take priority over subbrand treatment. Ambiguity becomes `possible_duplicate_needs_review`; overlapping historical domains or platform-specific social identities fail history validation.
