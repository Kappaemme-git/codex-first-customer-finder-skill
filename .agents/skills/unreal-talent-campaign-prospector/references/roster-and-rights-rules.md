# Roster and rights rules

No roster loaded: use a category or archetype only; do not name a real person, imply availability, or create a likeness concept; mark rights review required.

Approved roster loaded: named review requires explicit campaign verticals, talent categories, and channels. Deterministic validation checks the exact roster match, approval, reviewable availability, talent category, approved and restricted brand categories, territory, channels, unresolved exclusivity conflicts, and freshness. Duration and commercial requirements remain mandatory human review inputs. A fit is a recommendation, not a commitment.

Public images do not create commercial rights. Roster match, availability, campaign approval, and final asset approval are separate gates. Final legal and rights decisions belong to authorized humans.
