# Relationship and deduplication

Map brand, parent, subsidiaries, agency of record, media/influencer/talent agencies, production partners, and campaign. Preserve one commercial opportunity rather than separate leads for every source or participant.

Use shared statuses: `new_prospect`, `existing_no_new_trigger`, `existing_new_trigger`, `existing_active_outreach`, `existing_client`, `existing_partner`, `suppressed`, `possible_duplicate_needs_review`, `distinct_subbrand`, `parent_company_relationship`, and `agency_brand_overlap`. Existing companies are never new; re-engagement needs a distinct dated trigger, eligible cooldown, visible history, and explicit reason.
