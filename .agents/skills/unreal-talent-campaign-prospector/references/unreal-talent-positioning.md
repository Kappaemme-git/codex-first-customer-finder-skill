# Unreal Talent positioning

Unreal Talent qualifies brands and agencies that may buy licensed, talent-powered, AI-assisted campaigns with defined scope, territory, duration, channels, approvals, and brand-safety controls. It is not influencer list building.

Require a stronger commercial opportunity than UMG: meaningful campaign budget, current campaign trigger, talent-powered value, professional buying/legal path, likely rights readiness, and category compatibility.
