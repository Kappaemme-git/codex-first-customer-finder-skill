# Brand ideal customer profile

Prioritize consumer-facing brands with meaningful campaign maturity, credible licensing/production budget indicators, an active launch/partnership/event trigger, professional review processes, and products that benefit from trust, aspiration, culture, performance, identity, or fandom.

Downgrade ordinary gifting, weak budgets, no current use case, inactive marketing, prohibited/deceptive/unsafe categories, political or issue work without explicit policy, unsupported medical/financial/performance claims, unresolved controversy, exclusivity conflicts, and existing or suppressed relationships.
