# Evidence standard

Every qualified result needs a canonical domain, an official company or agency source, and at least one current fit, need, timing, campaign, or trigger signal. Preserve the original HTTP(S) URL and visible source date. Search snippets may locate evidence but do not qualify a prospect by themselves.

Use `observed` only for claims directly supported by the cited source. Use `inferred_high_confidence`, `inferred_low_confidence`, `unknown`, or `requires_internal_rights_check` for interpretation. Never present inferred budget, rights, availability, performance, or interest as fact.

Use public business information only. Do not bypass access controls, use paid enrichment, store private contact data, or infer sensitive traits.
